About us

Vought Project started with one developer and one phone: the Galaxy A51.

At the time, the A51 community was being written off by the biggest One UI porters, who saw the phone as not worth the effort. One developer disagreed and set out to bring the latest One UI to it anyway.

That was a year and a half ago. Today Vought Project runs on the A51, A12 and A35, backed by a team and a wiki that walks you through every install. The idea hasn’t changed since day one: an older Samsung still deserves the latest One UI update.

The team

Vought Project is created and maintained by a small group of developers, testers and designers. Plenty of others help along the way, including kernel providers, testers and bug reporters, and we appreciate their help!

Frequently asked questions

Will installing the ROM erase my data?

Yes. A clean flash is required on all three devices, and formatting data erases your apps, photos, messages and settings. Unlocking the bootloader also wipes the phone. Back up anything you want to keep before starting.

Do I need an unlocked bootloader and custom recovery?

Yes. Unlock the bootloader before installing a custom recovery. The A51 uses TWRP, the A12 uses OrangeFox or TWRP, and the A35 uses TWRP with the Fastboot installer. Follow the guide for your exact device rather than mixing steps between phones.

Why must I flash patched vbmeta before the recovery?

Android Verified Boot (AVB) can prevent a custom recovery from booting. Flash the patched vbmeta for your device before TWRP or OrangeFox, as explained in the wiki. Skipping this step can cause an AVB verification failure. The A12 guide already includes its vbmeta download link.

Can I install Vought Project over another custom ROM?

The current guides require a clean flash starting from stock firmware, not a dirty flash over another ROM. Follow your device guide for the required firmware and installation order. On the A35, the STANDARD Edition requires One UI 8 with the October 2025 security patch, and the CRYBON Edition requires One UI 8.5 with the May 2026 security patch.

Is a longer first boot normal?

The first boot after installation can take longer than usual. Let it finish without interrupting it. If the phone reports an AVB error or repeatedly restarts, check the patched vbmeta step and your device-specific instructions instead of assuming it is still completing the first boot.

What does Legacy or End of life mean?

Legacy editions are no longer maintained. They are listed separately from the current editions. Check that an edition is available for your exact device before using its installation guide.
